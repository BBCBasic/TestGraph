What can—and cannot—appear here

Public by deliberate choice

The server filters V2 records to visibility=public and publication_status=published. Public subject classification summaries may show status and model labels, but omit client IDs, private evidence and owner data.

Private by default

Private, unlisted and aggregate-only experiences do not appear. Deliberations remain private unless a separate sanitised case study is explicitly approved for publication.
